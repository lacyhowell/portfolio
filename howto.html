Before You Hit the Trail

Hiking does not have to be complicated. A little planning can make a first hike more comfortable, organized, and enjoyable. This guide covers five basic steps: choosing a trail, checking conditions, packing essentials, dressing for the weather, and sharing your plan.

Five Simple Steps

1

Choose an Appropriate Trail

Start with a short, clearly marked trail that matches your fitness level. Check the distance, elevation gain, and estimated time before leaving.

2

Check Weather and Trail Conditions

Look at the forecast and recent trail information. Avoid conditions that are beyond your experience, and remember that mountain weather can change quickly.

3

Pack the Basics

Bring water, snacks, sun protection, a simple first-aid kit, and a charged phone. A map or downloaded trail map is also useful if service is limited.

4

Dress for the Conditions

Wear comfortable shoes with good traction and use layers that can be added or removed. A light rain layer is useful when conditions may change.

5

Tell Someone Your Plan

Share the trail name, where you are parking, and when you expect to return. This simple step gives someone useful information if your plans unexpectedly change.

Quick Packing Reminder

  • Water and snacks
  • Trail map or navigation app
  • Sun protection
  • Basic first-aid supplies
  • Weather-appropriate layers
  • Charged phone and emergency contact plan
